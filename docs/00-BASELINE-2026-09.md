| Month | Sessions | ATC sessions | Reached checkout | Completed | Raw CVR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

August sessions nearly doubled while add-to-carts fell 37%. Daily data shows 37K→73K sessions/day on
Aug 6–9 with flat ATC. **This is bot traffic**, not demand. Desktop CVR is 0.83% vs mobile 2.15%; "direct"
is 78% of sessions at 0.96% CVR. Raw CVR is therefore not a trustworthy KPI right now.
The agent reports **CVR engaged = orders ÷ ATC sessions** (Aug: 21.6%, Jul: 19.8%) and excludes bot days.

Checkout completion (completed ÷ reached checkout): May 27.7%, Jun 26.2%, Jul 27.9%, Aug 27.6%. Stable.
Abandoned checkouts in August: **16,210**. That is the single largest recoverable pool.

Traffic mix (90 days): direct 1.48M sessions @0.96%, search 232K @3.44%, social 157K @1.35%, email 22.6K @2.29%,
paid **700** @0.29%. Order attribution: 74% of orders have a blank referrer; "email" shows 11 orders in 90 days
while email sessions imply ~500. **Attribution is broken (no UTMs), and paid is effectively off or untagged.**
