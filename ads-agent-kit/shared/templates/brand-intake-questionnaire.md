# Brand intake questionnaire (15 minutes with the owner)

The agent asks only the questions it could not answer from data (`10-brand-ingestion.md` Step 1). Ask in
batches of 3–5. Record answers verbatim in the brand playbook §15 Sources.

## Goals and money
1. What is the one business result you want from advertising in the next 90 days?
2. What monthly ad budget is approved today? What is the most you'd spend if returns hold? (cap = new-money line)
3. What does a customer/deal need to be worth for an ad dollar to be "worth it"? (target ROAS / CPA / cost per SQL)
4. Gross margin and fulfillment costs (or ACV, sales cycle, win rate for B2B)?

## Customer
5. Describe your best customers. Who buys most, most often, at best margin? (B2B: industries, company size, titles)
6. Who should we **never** target or spend on?
7. What do customers say right before they buy? What almost stops them?

## Offer and brand
8. Hero products/services to push? Anything we must never advertise (MAP, low stock, low margin)?
9. Words, claims or imagery we must use / must never use? Any legal/regulatory restrictions?
10. Who are your top 3 competitors and what do they do better/worse than you?

## History
11. What advertising have you tried — what worked, what failed, why do you think so?
12. Any seasonal peaks, launches, events or promotions in the next 12 months?

## Control
13. Who approves changes? Who else should get the daily update, and where (Slack/email/text)?
14. Is there anything you want the agent to do **without asking** (standing orders)? Anything it must **always** ask about?
15. Should the agent have an emergency brake (pause a single runaway entity, report within the hour)?
